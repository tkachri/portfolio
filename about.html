Personal Profile

Psychology + Management (STEM), Boston University

I've built products across UX research, venture building, and physical product design, from a marina booking platform that won Best Client Work to an AI proptech startup I'm currently building at Innovate BU.

I started in behavioral science because I wanted to understand why people make the decisions they make. I studied across five countries, tried consulting and marketing, and kept finding myself drawn to the work with real ownership, where you could see the direct line between your decisions and someone's experience. I came back to BU on a full merit scholarship for my MS in Management, and through real client engagements with Fischer Sports and NEREID I fell in love with product and UX. In the past six months, I co-founded LeaseLink, an AI proptech marketplace I'm actively building through Innovate BU, independently shipped CareGuard end to end, and designed Mote, a wearable concept that rethinks how intelligence sits on the body. I'm drawn to consumer-facing roles, products people choose to engage with, not ones they're stuck with.
